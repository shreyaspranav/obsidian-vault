## What is it?
"BitBeat" is a compact, battery powered, micro-controller based, portable music player.

## Requirements

- **Absolutely required**
	- Should be powered by a LiPo battery and should feature a charging circuit
	- Should support mainly 320kbps MP3 files
	- Should feature an microSD card to store all the music files
	- Should feature atleast 5h of continuous music playback
